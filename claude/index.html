Trip Planner

Use your trips in Claude

Connect Claude to Trip Planner and you can ask about your itinerary in plain words, or have Claude add bookings for you. It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

Before you start

  • A Trip Planner account. The same email and password you use in the app.
  • Claude access turned on for you. Ask Mario. Until he does, sign-in will say access isn't enabled.
  • A Claude account at claude.ai. The free plan works.

Connect it

  1. Copy this address.
    https://suzoxyptffuzjgndzukm.supabase.co/functions/v1/trip-agent/mcp
  2. Open Claude's connector settings: claude.ai/customize/connectors. Do this on a computer or in your phone's web browser — once it's connected, it works in the Claude app too.
  3. Click + Add, then Add custom connector
  4. Type Trip Planner as the name, paste the address into the URL box, and click Continue or Add. Leave the other settings as they are. If Claude asks how to identify itself, choose Register automatically.
  5. A Trip Planner page opens. Sign in with your Trip Planner email and password, then click Allow. You'll land back in Claude, connected.
  6. In a chat, click + at the bottom left, open Connectors, and switch on Trip Planner.

Things to try

  • “What's my itinerary for Japan, day by day?”
  • “When do I check out of the hotel in Kyoto?”
  • “Which nights don't have a hotel yet?”
  • “Add a dinner reservation at Sushi Saito in Tokyo on Jan 3 at 7:30pm for 2.”
  • If you've also connected Gmail in Claude: “Find my hotel confirmation emails for Osaka and add them to my Japan trip.”

Good to know

Anything Claude adds or changes is marked “Needs review” in the app until you open it and save it yourself, so nothing slips in unnoticed.

Times are local. A 3:00 PM check-in in Kyoto stays 3:00 PM, wherever you are when you look.

Claude sees the same trips you see in the app — your own and any shared with you — and nothing else.

To disconnect, remove Trip Planner from Claude's connector settings.

Stuck? If sign-in says access isn't enabled, ask Mario to turn it on. If something else goes wrong, send him a screenshot of the screen you're on.
